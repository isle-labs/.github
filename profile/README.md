Real-world credit is fragmented, paper-based, and slow — whether it's a supplier waiting 90 days to get paid, or an individual selling their crypto just to cover a bill. **ISLE brings real-world credit on-chain**, turning receivables and crypto holdings into instant, usable liquidity.

Two products, one credit layer:

|  | **Isle Enterprise** | **Isle Personal** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What it is** | The first on-chain credit marketplace for supply chain finance | Crypto-backed credit for individuals |
| **Who it's for** | Global businesses & their suppliers/buyers | Crypto holders who need liquidity without selling |
| **How it works** | Unlock liquidity from receivables — sellers get paid instantly, buyers extend payment terms | Borrow against your crypto, repay on credit-card-style cycles instead of continuous interest accrual |

> Learn more about ISLE 👉 [isle.finance](https://isle.finance/)
